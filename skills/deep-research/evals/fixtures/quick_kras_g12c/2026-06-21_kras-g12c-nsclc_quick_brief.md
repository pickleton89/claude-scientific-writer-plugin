# Clinical Efficacy of KRAS G12C Inhibitors in NSCLC — Quick Brief

## Key Findings

Two KRAS G12C inhibitors have Phase II efficacy data in previously treated,
KRAS G12C–mutated non–small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Sotorasib produced an
objective response rate (ORR) of 37.1% in pretreated patients
[T2: Skoulidis 2021, PMID:34096690]. Adagrasib produced an ORR of 42.9% among
evaluable patients [T2: Jänne 2022, PMID:35658005]. Both pivotal trials were
single-arm / non-randomized, so the cross-trial ORR difference must not be read
as evidence of comparative superiority.

## Limitations

Evidence is limited to single-arm Phase II trials; no head-to-head randomized
comparison exists. Acquired-resistance mechanisms and combination strategies are
out of scope for this quick brief.

Decision Tree: Biological & Drug Research

Workflow 7: Target Validation Pipeline

The canonical six-step pipeline from decision tree entry to optional manuscript output.

1
entity-resolution
Resolve gene / drug / disease identifiers — UniProt, Ensembl, ChEMBL, etc.
↓
2
target-intelligence
Build 12-section target dossier across 6 stages — expression, PPI, pathways, druggability, structure, variants.
↓
3
drug-intelligence
Build 10-section drug dossier across 6 stages — pharmacology, ADMET, clinical trials, indications.
↓
4
target-validation
Score 0–100 across 5 dimensions, assign T1–T4 evidence tier, produce GO / NO-GO recommendation.
↓
5
deep-research (optional)
Deep-dive lit-review mode on specific evidence gaps identified in validation.
↓
6
scientific-writing (optional)
Draft target assessment manuscript from dossier and validation data.
Dossier-first principle: Downstream output skills (plotting-libraries, scientific-schematics, peer-review, etc.) extract data from upstream dossiers when available, avoiding redundant ToolUniverse queries. Data-enriched requests route to the OUTPUT skill, not back to bio research skills, since the output skill controls rendering.

Workflow 8: Data-Enriched Research Output

When a downstream output skill needs biological data, the pipeline resolves entities and builds dossiers first, then hands off to whichever output skill the user actually needs.

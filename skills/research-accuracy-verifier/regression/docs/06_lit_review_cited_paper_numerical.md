## 2. Single-arm phase II evidence for pembrolizumab + platinum-doublet

Recent single-arm phase II studies have begun to characterize the benefit of adding platinum-doublet chemotherapy to pembrolizumab in PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC. Smith et al. reported a 12-month progression-free survival rate of **47.3%** (95% CI 38.1–55.9%) in their single-center investigator-initiated trial, exceeding the prespecified 40% threshold (Smith 2024).

In the same study, **210 patients** were enrolled with previously untreated PD-L1–high disease between January 2022 and November 2023 (Smith 2024). The reported median PFS of 11.2 months substantially exceeds the historical 7.7-month median for pembrolizumab monotherapy, and the difference reached statistical significance (p < 0.001 by one-sample log-rank against the historical control) (Smith 2024).

These results are encouraging but should be interpreted with caution given the single-arm design, single-center conduct, and absence of randomized comparison.

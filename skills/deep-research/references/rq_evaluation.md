# Research Question Evaluation Scoring

Lightweight, informational rubric for research questions. Applied during Phase 1
(Scoping) after output contract generation, before Checkpoint 1. Does NOT gate
progression — complements the advocate's answerability/scope/methodology review.

## Scoring Dimensions

### Significance (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Breakthrough potential with field-wide impact |
| 3 | Valuable question with a clear audience |
| 1 | Trivial question with minimal relevance |

Consider: academic impact, practical value, audience size.

### Novelty (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Entirely new problem or breakthrough methodology |
| 3 | New perspective or method combination on known problem |
| 1 | Duplicates or trivially extends existing work |

Consider: problem novelty, method novelty, perspective novelty.

### Feasibility (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Fully feasible with current technology and available resources |
| 3 | Challenging but manageable with focused effort |
| 1 | Infeasible with current technology or resources |

Consider: technical feasibility, resource availability, time constraints.

## Decision Matrix

| Significance | Novelty | Feasibility | Recommendation |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| High (4-5) | High (4-5) | High (4-5) | **Prioritize** -- strong candidate for immediate pursuit |
| High (4-5) | High (4-5) | Medium (3) | **Worth Attempting** -- high reward justifies the challenge |
| High (4-5) | Medium (3) | High (4-5) | **Safe Choice** -- lower novelty risk, reliable contribution |
| Medium (3) | High (4-5) | High (4-5) | **Worth Considering** -- novel approach may elevate significance |
| Low (1-2) | * | * | **Reconsider** -- revisit the research question's importance |

## Presentation

Present as a 4-row table (Dimension | Score | Notes) with the recommendation row
at the bottom. Include brief justification for each score.

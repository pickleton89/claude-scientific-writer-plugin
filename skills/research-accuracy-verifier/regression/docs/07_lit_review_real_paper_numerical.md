# Carboplatin/Nab-Paclitaxel Plus Pembrolizumab for Advanced NSCLC: A Brief Literature Review

*Synthesis prepared for an internal journal club. Section excerpt only.*

## 3. Phase I/II combination evidence for nab-paclitaxel-based regimens

The choice of platinum partner in pembrolizumab-based first-line regimens for advanced NSCLC remains an open question, and small phase I/II studies continue to add usable data. Gentzler and colleagues (Hoosier Cancer Research Network LUN13-175) reported the first prospective evaluation of carboplatin, nab-paclitaxel, and pembrolizumab in this setting (Gentzler 2024). The trial enrolled **46 evaluable patients** between June 2015 and July 2018 across four U.S. sites — 14 in phase I and 32 in phase II — and followed them for a median duration of **35.4 months** (Gentzler 2024).

In the intent-to-treat population, the regimen produced an objective response rate of **35%** and a median progression-free survival of **5.6 months** (95% CI 4.6–8.2) (Gentzler 2024). Median overall survival reached **19.4 months** (95% CI 12.4–28.1), which the authors note is consistent with the durable benefit observed in the phase III combination trials. The 12-month landmark progression-free survival was **23.6%** (95% CI 13.7%–40.5%) (Gentzler 2024).

The trial did not meet its prespecified co-primary efficacy endpoints, but the observed survival outcomes nevertheless support nab-paclitaxel as a viable platinum partner for pembrolizumab-based regimens, particularly given the inclusion of both squamous and nonsquamous histology.

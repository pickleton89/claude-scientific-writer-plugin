# Iconography & Illustration Library Licensing

> **Re-verify at each submission.** Terms last audited April 2026. BioRender revised its terms on 2026-01-16. Smart Servier Medical Art changed from CC-BY-3.0 to CC-BY-4.0 around 2023. Licensing for biomedical-illustration libraries changed materially in the 2023–2026 window, and pre-2024 blog posts on these terms are often wrong. Always check the provider's current page before first use in a paper.

Used by `paperbanana`, `research-posters`, `scientific-schematics`, `plotting-libraries`, and any other skill that composites icons from third-party libraries into publication figures.

## Quick reference

| Library | Primary license | Publication allowed? | Attribution required? | Commercial use? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BioRender** | Tiered (Basic / Academic Individual / Academic Lab / Academic Institutional; separate Industry/Business plans) | Tier-dependent | Yes (specific format) | Industry/Business plan required; check current BioRender terms |
| **NIH BIOART Source** | US public domain (+ CC subset) | Yes | Requested, not required | Yes |
| **SciDraw** | CC-BY-4.0 | Yes | Yes (citation; DOIs available) | Yes |
| **Smart Servier Medical Art** | CC-BY-4.0 (since ~2023) | Yes | Yes (specific format) | Yes, excluding Servier branding/logos |
| **Noun Project** | CC-BY-3.0 (free) / CC0 (subset) / Pro (royalty-free) | Yes, tier-dependent | Free tier: yes; Pro: no | Pro subscription or per-icon purchase |
| **Reactome** | CC-BY-4.0 (illustrations) + CC0 (annotation data) | Yes | Yes for CC-BY assets | Yes |
| **WikiPathways** | CC0 1.0 | Yes | Not legally required | Yes |
| **Somersault 18:24** | CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 | Academic only | Yes | **No** — not for for-profit textbooks or industry-sponsored materials |

## BioRender

**Tiered subscription model.** The free **Basic** tier allows educational and personal use only (posters, unpublished theses, grant applications) and carries a watermark on export; **publication in a peer-reviewed journal is prohibited** at Basic.

- **Academic Individual** subscriptions grant publication rights, but *only if the subscriber is listed as first, co-first, co-senior, or last author* on the paper.
- **Academic Lab** and **Academic Institutional** plans lift the author-position restriction.
- **Open-access export:** selecting the open-access option at export produces a **CC-BY-4.0 publication license** automatically — authors do not need to contact BioRender separately.
- **Cancellation:** publication licenses issued while the plan was active remain valid in perpetuity even after cancellation. New edits or republication after cancellation revert to Basic terms.
- **Accepted by:** Cell Press, Nature, Elsevier, eLife, PNAS, and the American Physiological Society (APS) routinely accept BioRender figures when the publication-license PDF is uploaded as a permission file.

**Attribution format (required):**
> Created in BioRender. Smith, J (2026) https://BioRender.com/xxxxxx.

**At submission:** upload the publication-license PDF as a permission file. Download it from the BioRender export dialog.

## NIH BIOART Source

**URL:** https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/

Launched October 2024 by the NIAID Visual and Medical Arts unit. Most of the ~2,000 vetted illustrations are in the **US public domain** — "free for any use, including educational, research, informational, and commercial purposes." A subset of entries carry a Creative Commons license that requires attribution; the metadata on each entry states which.

- **File formats provided:** EPS, AI, PNG, SVG.
- Attribution is *requested* even for public-domain entries.

**Attribution format (requested):**
> NIAID Visual & Medical Arts. [date]. [title]. NIAID NIH BioArt Source. bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/[id].

## SciDraw

**URL:** https://scidraw.io/

Founded by Federico Claudi and Alex Harston. All uploads licensed **CC-BY-4.0**. Commercial use (including for-profit journals) is permitted. Many drawings carry DOIs to simplify referencing.

**Attribution format (required):**
> [Creator name]. [Title]. SciDraw. [DOI or URL].

---

## Smart Servier Medical Art

**URL:** https://smart.servier.com/

Re-licensed as **CC-BY-4.0** around 2023 (previously CC-BY-3.0). Sharing, adaptation, remixing permitted in any medium including commercial use, with attribution. **Servier's own branding and logos are not covered** by the CC license.

**Attribution format (required):**
> Image provided by Servier Medical Art (https://smart.servier.com/), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

---

## Noun Project

**URL:** https://thenounproject.com/

Most individual icons are **CC-BY-3.0** with mandatory attribution; a subset are **CC0** (no attribution). A royalty-free option is available via **Icon Pro** or **Creator Pro** subscription, or per-icon purchase (~$4.99).

- **Free tier:** CC-BY-3.0 → inline attribution in figure caption is required.
- **Pro tier:** attribution not required.

**Attribution format (CC-BY-3.0 free tier):**
> [Icon name] by [Creator name] from the Noun Project (CC BY 3.0).

For commercial or industry figures, Pro is the cleaner choice because it removes the attribution obligation entirely.

---

## Reactome

**URL:** https://reactome.org/

Pathway illustrations, icon library, and branding are **CC-BY-4.0**. Annotation data (the underlying pathway graphs, gene sets) are **CC0 1.0** — no attribution legally required, though a citation to the Reactome publication is considered good practice.

**Attribution format (illustrations, CC-BY-4.0):**
> [Illustration title]. Reactome (https://reactome.org/), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

---

## WikiPathways

**URL:** https://www.wikipathways.org/

All content released under **CC0 1.0**. No attribution is legally required, but citing the WikiPathways publication is good practice when a pathway diagram is a substantive part of a figure.

---

## Somersault 18:24 — Library of Science and Medical Illustrations

**URL:** https://www.somersault1824.com/resources/

Licensed under **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. Usable in academic non-commercial figures. **Not safe** for for-profit textbooks, industry-sponsored materials, or any for-profit commercial use. The ShareAlike clause also means derivative works must be released under the same license — this can create friction for figures destined for subscription journals.

**If in doubt, prefer Servier, NIH BIOART, or SciDraw** (all permit commercial use).

## Pre-submission checks

- [ ] Every icon in the figure has an identified source.
- [ ] BioRender publication-license PDF downloaded and ready to upload as a permission file.
- [ ] Attribution lines drafted for the figure caption or Acknowledgments.
- [ ] No CC-BY-NC-SA assets (Somersault) in figures destined for for-profit or industry-sponsored publication.
- [ ] Servier branding/logos removed — only the illustrations are CC-licensed.
- [ ] Terms re-verified on each provider's current page (especially BioRender, whose terms change).

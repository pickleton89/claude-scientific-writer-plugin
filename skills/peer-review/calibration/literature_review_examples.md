# Literature Review Scoring Calibration

> Reference examples for consistent literature review scoring (1-5 scale).
> Weight: 15% of overall score.

---

## Score 5 Example (Excellent)

**Excerpt:**
> "Metabolic reprogramming is a hallmark of cancer (Hanahan & Weinberg, 2011). Glycolytic enzymes, particularly PFKFB3, have emerged as key regulators of the Warburg effect (Chesney et al., 2015; Yalcin et al., 2014). While PFKFB3 inhibition shows promise in preclinical models (Lu et al., 2019; Clem et al., 2008), clinical translation has been limited by off-target effects (Boyd et al., 2022). Recent structural studies (Zhang et al., 2021) offer new opportunities for selective targeting. However, whether PFKFB3 inhibition can overcome therapeutic resistance in TNBC remains unexplored, representing a critical knowledge gap that this study addresses."

**Why Score 5:**
- Establishes foundational concepts with seminal references
- Traces evolution of the field chronologically
- Synthesizes multiple studies into coherent narrative
- Acknowledges limitations/controversies (off-target effects)
- Clearly identifies knowledge gap
- Connects gap to current study's rationale
- Balanced mix of classic and recent citations

**Key Indicators:**
- ✓ Seminal papers cited
- ✓ Recent literature (within 2-3 years)
- ✓ Synthesis, not just listing
- ✓ Gap clearly articulated
- ✓ Both supporting and challenging evidence

## Score 4 Example (Good)

**Excerpt:**
> "PFKFB3 is a glycolytic enzyme implicated in cancer metabolism (Chesney et al., 2015). Several studies have shown that PFKFB3 inhibition reduces tumor growth (Lu et al., 2019; Clem et al., 2008). In breast cancer, glycolytic reprogramming is particularly important (Payen et al., 2020). The role of PFKFB3 in triple-negative breast cancer has not been fully characterized."

**Why Score 4:**
- Key references present
- Relevant context provided
- Gap identified but not fully motivated
- Missing synthesis across studies
- Some recent literature
- Could better integrate conflicting evidence

**Key Indicators:**
- ✓ Key papers cited
- ✓ Gap mentioned
- ○ Limited synthesis
- ○ Missing contrary evidence
- ○ Could be more comprehensive

---

## Score 3 Example (Adequate)

**Excerpt:**
> "Cancer cells rely on glycolysis for energy. PFKFB3 is an important enzyme in this process. Previous studies have examined PFKFB3 in various cancers. Little is known about PFKFB3 in breast cancer."

**Why Score 3:**
- General statements without citations
- Vague references ("previous studies")
- Gap stated but not justified
- Missing key foundational papers
- No synthesis of findings
- Functional but superficial

**Key Indicators:**
- ○ Few specific citations
- ○ Vague gap statement
- ○ No synthesis
- ○ Missing seminal works
- ○ Superficial coverage

---

## Score 2 Example (Needs Work)

**Excerpt:**
> "PFKFB3 has been studied before. It is important in cancer metabolism. We will study it in breast cancer because it has not been done."

**Why Score 2:**
- No citations
- Unsupported claims ("has been studied before")
- Gap is assertion, not demonstration
- No context provided
- Cannot evaluate novelty

**Key Indicators:**
- ✗ No citations
- ✗ Unsupported claims
- ✗ No justification
- ✗ Cannot assess novelty

---

## Score 1 Example (Poor)

**Excerpt:**
> "PFKFB3 is important. We studied it."

**Why Score 1:**
- No literature review present
- No context for the study
- Cannot evaluate contribution
- Complete revision required

**Key Indicators:**
- ✗ No literature review
- ✗ No citations
- ✗ No rationale

## Citation Coverage Checklist

For a complete literature review, verify:

### Foundational Works (Required)
- [ ] Seminal papers establishing the field
- [ ] Key methodological papers
- [ ] Landmark findings being built upon

### Recent Literature (Required)
- [ ] Studies from past 2-3 years
- [ ] Current state of the art
- [ ] Emerging questions/controversies

### Breadth (Important)
- [ ] Related work from adjacent fields
- [ ] Alternative approaches
- [ ] Negative/null findings

### Balance (Important)
- [ ] Both supporting and challenging evidence
- [ ] Acknowledgment of limitations
- [ ] Fair representation of controversies

---

## Red Flags for Low Scores

| Issue | Score Cap |
|-------|-----------|
| No citations in introduction | 2 |
| Only self-citations | 3 |
| No papers from past 5 years | 3 |
| Missing seminal works | 3 |
| Ignores contrary evidence | 3 |
| Gap not clearly stated | 3 |

---

## Scoring Decision Tree

```
Are there appropriate citations throughout?
├── No → Score 1-2
└── Yes → Are seminal AND recent works cited?
    ├── No → Score 3
    └── Yes → Is there synthesis and critical evaluation?
        ├── No → Score 3-4
        └── Yes → Is the gap clearly motivated?
            ├── No → Score 4
            └── Yes → Score 5
```

## Abstract

**Background.** PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous non–small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) is currently treated with pembrolizumab monotherapy. The addition of platinum-doublet chemotherapy may extend benefit.

**Methods.** Single-center, single-arm, investigator-initiated phase II trial. We enrolled 187 patients with previously untreated PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC between January 2022 and November 2023. Pembrolizumab 200 mg + carboplatin AUC 5 + pemetrexed 500 mg/m² q3w × 4 cycles, followed by pembrolizumab + pemetrexed maintenance.

**Results.** At a median follow-up of 18.4 months, the 12-month progression-free survival rate was 47.3% (95% CI 38.1–55.9%). Median PFS was 11.2 months (95% CI 8.4–14.7). Objective response rate was 51.6% (95% CI 42.4–60.8%). Compared with a historical median PFS of 7.7 months for pembrolizumab monotherapy in this population, the observed benefit reached statistical significance (p < 0.001 by one-sample log-rank against the historical control). Grade ≥ 3 adverse events occurred in 38% of treated patients.

**Conclusions.** Pembrolizumab plus carboplatin/pemetrexed produced a 12-month PFS rate of 47.3% in PD-L1–high non-squamous NSCLC, exceeding the prespecified 40% threshold. These findings support a randomized confirmatory trial.

# Pembrolizumab Plus Standard Chemotherapy in PD-L1–High Non–Small Cell Lung Cancer: A Single-Center Phase II Study

*Slide deck — internal lab meeting, draft. 12 slides.*

---

## Slide 1 — Title

Pembrolizumab plus carboplatin/pemetrexed in PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC: investigator-initiated phase II results.

---

## Slide 2 — Background

PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC has historically been treated with pembrolizumab monotherapy, with median overall survival (mOS) of approximately 26 months in registrational trials. The addition of platinum-doublet chemotherapy to anti-PD-1 therapy may extend benefit, particularly in patients with high tumor burden.

---

## Slide 3 — Hypothesis

Adding carboplatin/pemetrexed to pembrolizumab will improve progression-free survival (PFS) versus pembrolizumab alone in PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC, without prohibitive added toxicity.

---

## Slide 4 — Study Design

Single-center, single-arm, phase II investigator-initiated trial. Enrollment opened January 2022, closed November 2023. Primary endpoint: 12-month PFS rate. Secondary endpoints: ORR, mOS, grade ≥ 3 adverse events.

**We enrolled n = 120 patients with previously untreated PD-L1–high (TPS ≥ 50%) advanced non-squamous NSCLC** between January 2022 and November 2023.

---

## Slide 5 — Eligibility

Key inclusion: stage IV non-squamous NSCLC, PD-L1 TPS ≥ 50% by 22C3 IHC, ECOG PS 0–1, no prior systemic therapy for advanced disease, no driver mutation requiring targeted therapy. Key exclusion: active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppression, untreated CNS metastases.

---

## Slide 6 — Treatment

Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV q3w + carboplatin AUC 5 IV q3w × 4 cycles + pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV q3w, followed by pembrolizumab + pemetrexed maintenance until progression, intolerable toxicity, or 35 cycles total of pembrolizumab.

---

## Slide 7 — Patients (CONSORT)

**Of the 132 patients enrolled, 124 received at least one cycle of study therapy (intent-to-treat population).** Eight patients were excluded prior to first dose (4 withdrew consent, 3 had screening-detected ineligibility, 1 was lost to follow-up).

Median age 67 (range 41–84). 58% male. ECOG 0 in 41%, ECOG 1 in 59%. Stage IVA 22%, IVB 78%.

---

## Slide 8 — Primary Endpoint: 12-Month PFS

12-month PFS rate: 47.3% (95% CI 38.1–55.9%). Median PFS: 11.2 months (95% CI 8.4–14.7).

**Compared with the historical mPFS of 7.7 months for pembrolizumab monotherapy in this population, the observed difference reached statistical significance (p < 0.001 by one-sample log-rank against the historical control).**

---

## Slide 9 — Secondary Endpoints

ORR: 51.6% (95% CI 42.4–60.8%). DCR: 78.2%.

Median OS: not reached at data cutoff (median follow-up 18.4 months). 18-month OS rate: 71.4% (95% CI 62.9–78.4%).

---

## Slide 10 — Comparison vs Historical Control

**The PFS improvement over the 7.7-month historical median for pembrolizumab monotherapy was statistically significant (p < 0.01, one-sample log-rank).** The point estimate of mPFS difference was 3.5 months.

---

## Slide 11 — Safety

Grade ≥ 3 AEs in 38% of treated patients. Most common: neutropenia (14%), anemia (8%), immune-mediated pneumonitis (5%), fatigue (4%). Two treatment-related deaths (1 pneumonitis, 1 sepsis).

---

## Slide 12 — Conclusions

In this single-center phase II study of pembrolizumab plus carboplatin/pemetrexed in PD-L1–high advanced non-squamous NSCLC:

- 12-month PFS rate of 47.3% exceeded the prespecified 40% threshold.
- Safety profile was consistent with prior reports of the combination.
- These findings support a randomized confirmatory trial.

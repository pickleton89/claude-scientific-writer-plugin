# Oligon Scientific — Methodology Diagram Style Guide

## 1. The "Oligon Look"

The Oligon aesthetic is clean, professional scientific minimalism. White backgrounds
with Brand Blue (#2DB2E8) as the primary accent and Contrast Orange (#E8622D) used
sparingly for emphasis. The design prioritizes clarity and readability over decoration.

## 2. Detailed Style Options

### A. Color Palette

Design Philosophy: Use color to communicate meaning, not to decorate.
Restrict to the Oligon palette for brand consistency.

Primary Colors:
- Brand Blue (#2DB2E8): Primary elements, highlights, key components, active modules,
  flow arrows, process boxes. This is the dominant accent color.
- Contrast Orange (#E8622D): Use sparingly for alerts, warnings, opposing elements,
  or single points of emphasis. Maximum 1-2 elements per figure.

Neutral Colors:
- Dark Gray (#222222): All text, labels, titles, annotations.
- Medium Gray (#666666): Secondary/supporting elements, borders, dividers.
- Light Gray (#F5F5F5): Subtle background fills for grouping regions.
- White (#FFFFFF): Primary background. Always white.

Functional Element Colors:
- Active/Primary modules: Brand Blue (#2DB2E8) fill or border.
- Secondary/Supporting modules: Light gray (#F5F5F5) fill with gray (#666666) border.
- Highlights/Emphasis: Contrast Orange (#E8622D), used for at most 1-2 elements.
- Data flow arrows: Dark Gray (#222222) or Brand Blue (#2DB2E8).
- Grouping containers: White or very light gray (#F5F5F5) with thin gray borders.

### B. Shapes & Containers

- Process Nodes: Rounded rectangles with Brand Blue fill or border.
- Data/Input/Output: Rectangles with thin gray borders.
- Grouping: Light gray (#F5F5F5) background regions with thin dashed gray borders.
- Containers: Minimal styling, white or near-white fill.

### C. Lines & Arrows

- Primary flow: Solid, Dark Gray (#222222) or Brand Blue (#2DB2E8), 1.5-2pt weight.
- Secondary flow: Dashed, Medium Gray (#666666), 1pt weight.
- Arrow style: Clean, simple arrowheads. No decorative arrowheads.
- Line routing: Orthogonal/elbow for architectures, curved for feedback loops.

### D. Typography

- All text: Sans-serif font family (Arial, Helvetica, Roboto).
- Titles: 14pt, bold, Dark Gray (#222222).
- Labels: 10pt, regular, Dark Gray (#222222).
- Annotations: 8pt, regular, Medium Gray (#666666).
- Minimum text size: 7pt at final reproduction size.
- Contrast ratio: Minimum 4.5:1 for all text against background.

### E. Layout & Composition

- Flow: Left-to-right for sequential processes, top-to-bottom for hierarchical.
- Background: Always white (#FFFFFF).
- Spacing: Generous whitespace between elements.
- Alignment: Snap to implicit grid, consistent spacing.
- Balance: Distribute visual weight evenly across the composition.
- Visual hierarchy: Use size, weight, and Brand Blue to guide the eye.

## 3. Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- Using colors outside the Oligon palette (no pastels, no saturated backgrounds).
- Red/green color combinations (colorblind unfriendly).
- Cluttered layouts or excessive decoration.
- Gradients or drop shadows unless functionally necessary.
- Text smaller than 7pt at final size.
- More than 5-6 distinct colors in a single figure.
- Heavy black outlines or "PowerPoint default" styling.
- Inconsistent line weights or arrow styles.

## 4. Brand Summary

The Oligon figure should feel: clean, professional, minimal, and immediately readable.
Brand Blue (#2DB2E8) is the signature color. When someone sees the figure, the blue
accent on a white background should be recognizable as Oligon.

# Emerging Approaches in CRISPR Base Editing for Hemoglobinopathies

## Background

Adenine base editors (ABEs) have been used to recreate naturally occurring
hereditary persistence of fetal hemoglobin (HPFH) variants in hematopoietic
stem and progenitor cells, offering a precise alternative to nuclease-based
disruption of *BCL11A*. A first-in-human application of ABE-corrected
autologous stem cells reported durable fetal hemoglobin induction with no
detectable off-target editing at 12 months of follow-up (Okonkwo et al., 2024,
*Nature Medicine*, https://doi.org/10.1093/oncolo/oyad180).

This result established base editing as a clinically viable strategy for the
β-hemoglobinopathies and motivated the multi-center trials now in progress.
